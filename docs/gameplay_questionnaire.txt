BOLO GAMEPLAY QUESTIONNAIRE
Answer under each question. Numbers are more useful than adjectives.
Skip anything you do not know; "don't know" is a fine answer.

--- TANKS ---

1. What is a tank's maximum armour?
Tanks die after 9 hits. Visually the game displays 8 armor, and then you die if you get hit while at 0.
But this is presumably equivalent to having 9, and you die when reaching 0.

2. How much armour does one shell hit remove from a tank?
One.

3. How much armour does driving over a mine remove from a tank?
In WinBolo, 2. That is probably (not 100% certain) the same in Bolo.

4. Does one "armour" drain from a base restore one point of tank armour?
I strongly believe so.

5. What is the maximum tank speed on each terrain type (road, grass, forest, swamp, crater, rubble, river, boat)?
The following numbers give the correct ratios but may not be in the correct units.
Road = 16. Grass = 12. Forest = 6. Swamp = 3. Crater = 3. Rubble = 3. River = 3.
Boats are not really travelled over but rather *in*. Tanks in boats move at 16.

6. What unit is the speed byte in a tank position packet, and what is its maximum value?
Don't know.

7. How fast does a tank turn (time for a full circle, or degrees per tick)?
I wouldn't trust an emulator. But in WinBolo, time for a full circle:
 - Road/grass/base/boat: 2.61 s
 - Forest: 5.17 s
 - River/swamp/crater/rubble: 10.29 s
Mac Bolo is probably similar. You could check a replay.

8. What is the minimum time between two shots from a tank?
Approximately a quarter second. Not exact. WinBolo has 260 ms.
A replay could tell the truth.

9. Can a tank fire while moving or turning?
Yes and yes.

10. When exactly does a tank count as hidden in trees (centre pixel in a forest square, or fully inside one)?
At the very least I believe you need the entire box of the tank inside, and it may be even stricter than that. Unsure.
Careful analysis of replays might show.

11. What does being hidden actually hide: the tank from enemy screens, from radar, from pillboxes, or all three?
From enemy screens and from pillboxes, yes. I do not know what you mean by "radar".

12. Is deep sea instant death, or only death when the tank has no boat?
Instant death when not in a boat. Harmless in a boat.

13. Where does a tank respawn: a random start square, the furthest from enemies, or the player's original one?
Unknown. Not always the original one.

14. What ammo, mines and armour does a tank respawn with in an open game? In a tournament game? In a strict game?
Here "max" means 8 or 9 armor, depending on how you count (see Q1).
Open: 40/40/max. Strict: 0/0/max. Tournament: variable shots, no mines, max armor.

--- SHELLS ---

15. Does a shell damage an allied tank?
Yes.

16. Does a shell damage a man (LGM)?
If the shell fall is very near him, or it hits a forest tile he is in, yes. One shot kills him.
Note that shells can fly over a man harmlessly if the above conditions aren't met.

17. Does a live pillbox stop a shell? Does a dead pillbox stop a shell?
Yes. No.

18. At what armour level does a base start blocking shells? Do friendly and neutral bases block too?
Not sure about the first question. Friendly and neutral bases do not block shells.

19. Does a shell detonate a mine?
Yes.

20. What does a shell do to a boat?
Destroys it, whether there is a tank in it or not.

--- PILLBOXES ---

21. How does a pillbox's fire rate rise when it is attacked, and how does it decay afterwards?
I believe the fire rate doubles on a hit.
In WinBolo, if the standard delay between shots is 100 (which I think is near 2 seconds) a fully angry pill has delay 6.
The implied 8 shots a second feels about right. This question could be resolved from corpus evidence.
It slowly decays to delay 100 again.

22. What are the fastest and slowest intervals between shots from one pillbox?
See Q21.

23. What does the "speed" byte in the pillbox list mean, and what values does it take?
Normally 6-100, see above.

24. Does a pillbox fire at a man (LGM)? At a tank in a boat?
No. Yes.

25. What decides whether the man repairs a pillbox by 4, 8, 12 or fully?
Not sure - I think possibly if the tank sending has less than a full unit of wood to spend.

26. What resource does a pillbox repair consume, and how much?
Wood. 1 tree harvested supplies exactly the amount needed to fully repair a pillbox, or plant a dead one at full health.

27. Can a pillbox be picked up by anyone once it is dead, including its owner?
Yes.

--- BASES ---

28. Can a hostile base be captured while it still has armour, or must it be at zero first?
It must be reduced to near-zero (which can be done by a hostile tank shooting it). I do not know if it must be exactly zero.

29. Is a neutral base captured on contact?
On driving over it.

30. Must the tank be stopped on a base to refuel, and how fast does each resource transfer?
Slow motion on the base is OK, as long as it stays on the square. In WinBolo, going from 0/0/0 to 40/40/9 takes about 20 seconds, which probably isn't far off the Mac Bolo values.

31. Does a base refuel a tank owned by an ally? By a neutral player?
Yes. There is no such thing as a neutral player.

32. Does a base do anything hostile to an enemy tank sitting on it?
It is impassable to an enemy tank until reduced in armor to the point where it can be captured by driving over it.

--- THE MAN (LGM) ---

33. What can the man build or change, and what does each action cost in wood?
Build or repair a pillbox, build a road, build a building, build a boat (only on river), harvest a tree (becomes grass) retrieving wood.
Pillbox 1, road 0.5 I think, boat is 5 I think, not sure about building.

34. How long does each action take (harvesting a tree, laying road, building a wall, building a boat, repairing a pill, planting a mine)?
It is very fast once the man is there. This could be determined from any replay.

35. How fast does the man walk, and does terrain change his speed?
Check replays. Yes.

36. Which terrain can the man cross? Can he board a boat?
He is blocked by everything that blocks a tank. I believe he can enter a boat, but not use it. I could be wrong about him entering it.

37. What kills the man: shells, mines, being driven over, anything else?
Shells (see Q16) and explosions on him. Possibly harvesting a tree with a hidden mine (but practically no games allow hidden mines).

38. How long does the parachute take to arrive, and where does the new man land?
The new man lands where the tank was at the moment of the LGM's death. For speed, check a replay.

39. What happens to a pillbox the man is carrying when he is killed?
It is dropped on the ground at 0 health, still owned by that player.

40. How does the man decide the path to his target, and what makes him give up?
A straight line, as follows:
Let's say he's going south east at (arbitrary units) speed [3,3]. If he encounters an obstacle to his east, his speed becomes [0,3].
If he encounters an obstacle to the south as well, he would now be at [0,0] at which point he gives up.

--- TERRAIN ---

41. What is the rule for forest regrowth: any grass, only grass next to forest, and at what rate?
In fact it is not limited to grass, and can go on most land tiles except impassable ones. Not sure about rate, check a replay.
It may depend on the number of players, probably each client is simulating its own tree growth, i.e. 4 players may have 4 times as much as one.

42. When a tank drives off a boat onto land, does the boat stay behind on the water square?
Yes.

43. Do rivers, swamps or craters slow or damage anything besides the tank?
Men are slowed by swamps and craters, and blocked by rivers.
Tanks can slowly cross river. This causes them to lose shells and mines slowly.

44. Can a crater be filled in, and by what?
Build a road on it. It also naturally floods if next to water.

--- MINES ---

45. Who can see a mine under each hidden-mines setting?
Unknown, but almost all games disallow hidden mines.

46. Can allies see each other's mines?
If hidden mines are allowed then I don't know.

47. Does the man detonate a mine by walking on it?
I believe not.

48. Can a mine be laid on any terrain, and can it be laid from a boat?
The FORMAT docs "Terrain" section lists the 6 terrains that can have mines.
It cannot be laid by the tank from a boat, but a man could be sent from the boat to plant a mine on land.
The man cannot swim, so the boat would have to be adjacent to land, though not necessarily to the plant square.

--- ALLIANCES AND PLAYERS ---

49. What does an alliance share beyond pills and bases: view, radar, messages, anything else?
It is possible to change your view so that instead of seeing the area around your tank, you see the area around any friendly pillbox.
Alliances can send messages that can only be seen by allies. I don't know what radar is. Nothing else I can think of.

50. When a player leaves an alliance, which member inherits his planted pillboxes and bases?
This is modelled in the code.

51. What did Join restore and what did Rejoin restore for a returning player?
Join: I believe nothing. Rejoin: hopefully modelled correctly in the code by now.

52. What happened to a player's pillboxes and bases while he was disconnected?
I think the same as if he had quit.

53. How is a "nearby" chat recipient decided?
I didn't know there was such a thing.

54. What differs between open, tournament and strict games besides the strict-game empty respawn?
Just how much ammo / mines a tank carries on respawn.

55. Do the start delay and time limit change anything visible in play?
I don't think so.

56. Did AI "brain" players behave in any way a log would show, such as never chatting or fixed patterns?
I believe they did send messages. Fixed patterns according to their own coding, probably.

57. What is recorded in the pill and base history string (the player@node name in the start-of-log groups)?
Don't know.
